Most writing about software architecture describes systems as they look on a whiteboard. This blog is about systems as they look in production: shaped by deadlines, budgets, team structure, and code that was written before anyone in the room joined.

## What "pragmatic" means here

Being pragmatic doesn't mean ignoring principles. It means:

- **Pick the simplest design that solves today's problem** and leaves room to change it tomorrow.
- **Make trade-offs explicit.** Every decision costs something, so write down what.
- **Prefer evolution to revolution.** Big-bang rewrites rarely deliver what they promise.
